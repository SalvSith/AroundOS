The original author specifies three parts for wall mounting and two additional parts for desk use. Print STL files in their supplied orientation without supports. Orient STEP geometry before slicing. The printed enclosure parts slide/click together without additional fasteners; attaching the Pi to the screen uses the supplied spacers and screws. The design is for the Waveshare round 5-inch display; it is not a general Raspberry Pi board enclosure.

## Illustrated assembly manual

- [Printable wireframe assembly manual (PDF)](Assembly%20Manual/AroundOS%20Assembly%20Manual.pdf)
- [Editable assembly guide](Assembly%20Manual/Assembly%20Guide.md)
- [Vector CAD illustrations](Assembly%20Manual/illustrations)

The 14-page neon-green guide covers parts, port identification, printing checks, HDMI/USB wiring, display installation, Pi attachment with the supplied spacers and screws, cover clearance, the desk stand, and first boot. Enclosure and screen views come from the supplied STEP files. The Raspberry Pi 4 and mounting hardware use simplified wire-style illustrations; hardware gaps are illustrative. Main power connects to the Pi; screen HDMI and USB-C Touch connect to Pi HDMI0 and USB-A respectively. Attach the Pi to the screen, connect HDMI and USB touch, and complete the bench test before preparing or fitting the enclosure parts. Physical fit, latch engagement, cable clearance and stand stability still need a build check. The screen model itself is not redistributed here.
